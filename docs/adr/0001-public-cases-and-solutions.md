# Publish benchmark cases and solutions immediately

On 2026-10-11, the user chose immediate publication of every authored case and solution rather than private authoring followed by staged development and confirmation releases. The suite therefore makes its tasks and repairs openly inspectable; future comparisons must acknowledge possible prior exposure and cannot claim a private or uncontaminated case holdout.

In Q19 the user also selected public acceptance checks, evaluator source and environment recipes. This publication policy does not change the accepted attempt boundary: agents receive fresh bugged workspaces without healthy parent history, reference repairs or grader material, and execution remains offline. Public availability and accessibility during a particular attempt are different properties; grader-only checks are not secret tests.
