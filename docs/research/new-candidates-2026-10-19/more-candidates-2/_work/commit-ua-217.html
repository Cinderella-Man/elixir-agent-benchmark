Sign and verify the state nonce cookie (#217)
The request phase now signs the state cookie, and the callback explicitly fetches and verifies it before checking the nonce. Unsigned or tampered cookies fail the state check before the provider callback runs. The release moves to 0.11.0, requires Plug 1.10 or later, and documents the stable host-provided secret required across requests and instances, along with the need to restart in-flight OAuth flows after upgrading. Tests cover signed-cookie round trips, previously fetched cookies, invalid cookies and state parameters, cookie deletion, and missing or invalid secrets. Fixes #153 Co-authored-by: Matt Van Horn <455140+mvanhorn@users.noreply.github.com>
