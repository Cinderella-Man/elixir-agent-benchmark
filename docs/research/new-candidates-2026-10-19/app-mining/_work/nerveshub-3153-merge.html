Take a deleted device out of its deployment group and disconnect it (#3153)
Deleting a device now takes it out of its deployment group and closes its connection. Before, a device deleted while connected stayed in its group, and the orchestrator kept trying to update it. ## What went wrong `Updates.build_available_devices_query/3` has no `deleted_at` filter, so a deleted device that was still connected was still picked for updates. - Past its failure threshold or rate, the coordinator tries to put it in the penalty box with `Updates.update_blocked_until/2`. `Device.changeset/2` refuses any write to a deleted device, the error is discarded, and the next run picks the device again. The orchestrator runs again every 3 seconds with no end, leaving a concurrency slot unused and writing an "automatically blocked firmware upgrades" audit log each time. - Otherwise it is sent the update, because `DeviceEvents.schedule_update/3` loads it with a plain `Repo.get`. ## Change - `Devices.delete_device/1` clears `deployment_id` in the soft delete's changeset, inside the existing transaction. - Once that commits, the new `DeviceEvents.deleted/1` broadcasts `disconnect` on `device_socket:<id>`, as moving a device to another product already does. `moved_product/1` now shares that code. - The device can't connect again while deleted. Certificate auth and device keys reject a deleted device, and a product key can't create a new device with its identifier. - A restored device has no deployment group until it next connects. It is then matched to one by the groups' conditions, like any device without a group, so a group it was put in by hand isn't restored. - The delete confirmation now says the device will be disconnected and removed from its deployment group, and `docs/device_authentication.md` has a section on deleted devices. 🤖 Generated with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) Co-authored-by: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com> Co-authored-by: Nate Shoemaker <nshoes@users.noreply.github.com>
