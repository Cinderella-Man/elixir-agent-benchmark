Give devices fresh update attempts after an orchestrator penalty (#3154)
A device the orchestrator puts in the penalty box now gets a full set of update attempts when the penalty ends. Before this, it was blocked again on the first pass after the penalty ran out, and kept being blocked until someone cleared it by hand. ## Why the penalty never ended There were two ways into the penalty box, and they disagreed about the device's attempts: - `verify_update_eligibility/3` used `put_device_in_penalty_box/3`, which clears `update_attempts`. - The orchestrator's `schedule_devices!` used `Updates.update_blocked_until/2`, which left them alone. `failure_threshold_met?/2` counts every attempt, with no time window. So a device the orchestrator blocked still met the threshold when its penalty ended. The next pass blocked it for another `penalty_timeout_minutes` and wrote another "automatically blocked firmware upgrades" audit log, and the same thing happened at the end of every penalty after that. Automatic updates nearly always take the orchestrator's path, because `schedule_devices!` checks the limits before `verify_update_eligibility/3` sees the device. ## One penalty function `Updates.enforce_failure_limits/2` checks the failure rate and threshold and penalizes the device through `put_device_in_penalty_box/3`. The orchestrator and `verify_update_eligibility/3` both call it, and `update_blocked_until/2` is gone. Other changes that come with it: - Orchestrator penalties are logged at info with their reason, as the other path's already were. - A penalty that can't be saved logs a warning, and the device is still refused the update. Before, `verify_update_eligibility/3` raised a `MatchError` and the orchestrator threw the error away. - The penalty log reads the firmware version with `get_in`. A device with no `current_device_firmware` raised `BadMapError` there, which would crash the orchestrator once its penalties went through this function. Tests didn't catch it: `config/test.exs` sets the log level to `:warning`, so the arguments to the info log were never evaluated. ## Workflow steps retry penalized devices A workflow step counts a device as failed while it is in the penalty box. Before, a device stayed there until someone cleared it. Now it is retried when its penalty ends, and doesn't count as failed while it retries. A step that has already failed stops scheduling devices, so it isn't affected. A step that is still running can complete if the device later updates, instead of waiting for someone to retry or skip it. `Workflows.retry_step/3` already clears attempts for the same reason when a person retries a step. 🤖 Generated with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) --------- Co-authored-by: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com> Co-authored-by: Nate Shoemaker <nshoes@users.noreply.github.com>
