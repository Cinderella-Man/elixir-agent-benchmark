fix(jobs): stop metadata refresh jobs snoozing forever
MetadataRefresh and AiringEpisodeRefresh snoozed a random startup delay whenever attempt == 1. Oban 2.24 rolls attempt back on snooze, so every wake-up was attempt 1 again and snoozed again. Neither job has completed since the 2026-09-12 dependency bump, which left aired episodes titled "TBA" and stopped the weekly metadata pass. Startup jitter now goes through Mydia.Jobs.StartupJitter, which reads meta["snoozed"]. A source guard fails any other snooze in lib/, and an end-to-end test drains a real Oban queue to prove the snooze ends. A migration cancels the stuck scheduled rows so they do not all wake at once against the relay.
